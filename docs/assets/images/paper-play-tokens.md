## Prompt final

Create ONE sprite sheet with TWO square cells side by side, precisely 2:1 landscape, equal halves. Attached reference is strict NeuroIA mascot and visual style reference. Make two round tactile play tokens, same scale, each centered in its square, diameter 88% of square, pure white background outside. Cute textured cut paper, risograph grain, imperfect dark teal ink outlines, same turquoise pear and lavender pebble character faces with long simple nose, curved smiling eyes. LEFT token: a round pale peach paper archery target with three thick hand-painted concentric rings in muted terracotta, cream and teal; exact middle is a clearly contrasting dark teal circular bullseye, small smiling eyes above and curved smile below the center, like a friendly paper character target. RIGHT token: a round lavender pebble companion smiling and waving with small hand fully inside circular pastel lilac paper token, dark teal simple eyes and nose, little turquoise heart on belly at exact center as touch anchor. Simple large readable silhouette, no tiny decoration. Both entire drawings contained inside round tokens so circular clipping is safe. Match supplied reference paper grain, chunky character language, muted palette. No metallic pin, no gloss, no photorealism, no 3D, no text, no numerals, no starbursts or shadows. White gutters minimal and equal. Output one image, not separate files.
